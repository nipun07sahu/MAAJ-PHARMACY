Our Editorial Principles

  • Medicine-related information is presented responsibly and should not replace professional medical advice.
  • Product information may include uses, precautions, directions, ingredients and other relevant details.
  • We do not intentionally publish misleading medical claims.
  • Information may be reviewed and updated when necessary.
  • Product availability, price and other commercial information may change without prior notice.
  • Customers should always read the medicine packaging, label and instructions before use.

The information available on the MAAJ PHARMACY website is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified doctor, pharmacist or other healthcare professional.

MAAJ PHARMACY does not guarantee that every health-related article or educational resource is suitable for every individual.

Returns & Refunds Policy

MAAJ PHARMACY aims to provide genuine and properly packed products to customers.

Return Eligibility

A return or replacement request may be considered where:

  • The wrong product was delivered.
  • The product was damaged during delivery.
  • The product received is defective or unusable.
  • The delivered product does not reasonably match the ordered product.

Medicines

For safety and regulatory reasons, opened, used or tampered medicines may generally not be eligible for return. Return eligibility may also depend on the product type and applicable legal requirements.

Reporting a Problem

Customers should contact MAAJ PHARMACY as soon as possible after delivery if there is an issue with an order.

Customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order details
  • Photographs of the product/package
  • Description of the issue
  • Other information reasonably required to investigate the complaint

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and applicable banking/payment procedures. For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund process may require additional information from the customer.

Non-Returnable Situations

  • The product has been used or opened.
  • The product has been damaged by the customer.
  • The packaging has been intentionally damaged.
  • The customer ordered the wrong product.
  • The return request is made after the applicable return period.
  • The product is not eligible for return due to applicable regulations.

MAAJ PHARMACY reserves the right to assess each return/refund request individually.

05

Medicine Delivery & Cancellations Policy

MAAJ PHARMACY provides medicine and healthcare-product delivery subject to product availability, delivery-area coverage and applicable requirements.

Delivery Area

Delivery is currently intended for customers within the serviceable delivery area of MAAJ PHARMACY. The website/order system may restrict orders outside the available delivery area.

Delivery Time

Our target is to provide delivery within 1 day for eligible orders in the serviceable area.

However, delivery time may vary because of:

  • Product availability
  • Prescription verification
  • Weather conditions
  • Local transportation issues
  • Customer availability
  • Incorrect delivery information
  • Unforeseen operational circumstances

Therefore, a delivery estimate should not be treated as an unconditional guarantee in circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Delivery Address

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address and reachable mobile number. MAAJ PHARMACY is not responsible for delays caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.

Order Cancellation

An order may be cancelled before dispatch, subject to the order status and applicable circumstances. After dispatch, cancellation may not always be possible.

MAAJ PHARMACY may cancel an order if:

  • The requested product is unavailable.
  • A required prescription is not provided or cannot be verified.
  • The delivery address is outside the serviceable area.
  • There is a suspected fraudulent transaction.
  • There is an incorrect price or product listing.
  • Applicable law or regulatory requirements prevent fulfilment.

Failed Delivery

If delivery cannot be completed because the customer is unavailable, unreachable or has provided an incorrect address, MAAJ PHARMACY may contact the customer to arrange another delivery attempt. Additional delivery restrictions or charges may apply where applicable.

Prescription & Restricted Medicines Policy

MAAJ PHARMACY follows applicable requirements relating to prescription and restricted medicines.

Prescription Requirement

Certain medicines can only be supplied when a valid prescription from a qualified medical practitioner is provided and accepted where required by applicable law. MAAJ PHARMACY may request prescription details or a copy/image of the prescription before processing an order.

Prescription Verification

A prescription may be checked for relevant information such as:

  • Patient details
  • Medicine name
  • Strength
  • Dosage instructions
  • Quantity
  • Prescriber information
  • Date
  • Other legally required information

Order Rejection

MAAJ PHARMACY may refuse or cancel an order if:

  • No required prescription is provided.
  • The prescription is unclear or incomplete.
  • The prescription appears altered or invalid.
  • The medicine or quantity requested is not consistent with applicable requirements.
  • The medicine is unavailable.
  • Supplying the medicine would violate applicable law or regulations.

Restricted Medicines

Certain medicines may be subject to additional restrictions regarding sale, quantity, prescription, storage, record keeping or delivery. MAAJ PHARMACY will follow applicable requirements for such products.

Customer Responsibility

Customers must not provide false, altered or fraudulent prescriptions. Customers must use medicines only as directed by a qualified healthcare professional and according to the medicine's approved instructions.

Important Medical Notice

MAAJ PHARMACY does not encourage self-medication with prescription medicines. If you are unsure whether a medicine is appropriate for you, consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before using it.
