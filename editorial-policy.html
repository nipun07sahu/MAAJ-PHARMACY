Editorial Policy

Effective Date: 2 September 2026

At MAAJ PHARMACY, we aim to provide clear, useful and responsible health-related information to our customers.

Our health articles, medicine information, product descriptions and educational content are prepared for general informational and educational purposes.

Our Editorial Principles

  • We aim to provide accurate and understandable information.
  • Medicine-related information is presented responsibly and should not replace professional medical advice.
  • Product information may include uses, precautions, directions, ingredients and other relevant details.
  • We do not intentionally publish misleading medical claims.
  • Information may be reviewed and updated when necessary.
  • Product availability, price and other commercial information may change without prior notice.
  • Customers should always read the medicine packaging, label and instructions before use.
  • Customers should consult a qualified doctor or healthcare professional when medical advice, diagnosis or treatment is required.
Medical Disclaimer

The information available on the MAAJ PHARMACY website is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified doctor, pharmacist or other healthcare professional.

MAAJ PHARMACY does not guarantee that every health-related article or educational resource is suitable for every individual.
