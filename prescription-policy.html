MAAJ PHARMACY

Prescription & Restricted Medicines Policy

Effective Date: 2 September 2026

MAAJ PHARMACY follows applicable requirements relating to prescription and restricted medicines.

Prescription Requirement

Certain medicines can only be supplied when a valid prescription from a qualified medical practitioner is provided and accepted where required by applicable law. MAAJ PHARMACY may request prescription details or a copy/image of the prescription before processing an order.

Prescription Verification

A prescription may be checked for relevant information such as:

  • Patient details
  • Medicine name
  • Strength
  • Dosage instructions
  • Quantity
  • Prescriber information
  • Date
  • Other legally required information

Order Rejection

MAAJ PHARMACY may refuse or cancel an order if:

  • No required prescription is provided.
  • The prescription is unclear or incomplete.
  • The prescription appears altered or invalid.
  • The medicine or quantity requested is not consistent with applicable requirements.
  • The medicine is unavailable.
  • Supplying the medicine would violate applicable law or regulations.

Restricted Medicines

Certain medicines may be subject to additional restrictions regarding sale, quantity, prescription, storage, record keeping or delivery. MAAJ PHARMACY will follow applicable requirements for such products.

Customer Responsibility

Customers must not provide false, altered or fraudulent prescriptions. Customers must use medicines only as directed by a qualified healthcare professional and according to the medicine's approved instructions.

Important Medical Notice

MAAJ PHARMACY does not encourage self-medication with prescription medicines. If you are unsure whether a medicine is appropriate for you, consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before using it.
