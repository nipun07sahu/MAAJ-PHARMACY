MAAJ PHARMACY

Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective Date: 2 September 2026

MAAJ PHARMACY aims to provide genuine and properly packed products to customers.

Return Eligibility

A return or replacement request may be considered where:

  • The wrong product was delivered.
  • The product was damaged during delivery.
  • The product received is defective or unusable.
  • The delivered product does not reasonably match the ordered product.

Medicines

For safety and regulatory reasons, opened, used or tampered medicines may generally not be eligible for return. Return eligibility may also depend on the product type and applicable legal requirements.

Reporting a Problem

Customers should contact MAAJ PHARMACY as soon as possible after delivery if there is an issue with an order.

Customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order details
  • Photographs of the product/package
  • Description of the issue
  • Other information reasonably required to investigate the complaint

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and applicable banking/payment procedures. For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund process may require additional information from the customer.

Non-Returnable Situations

  • The product has been used or opened.
  • The product has been damaged by the customer.
  • The packaging has been intentionally damaged.
  • The customer ordered the wrong product.
  • The return request is made after the applicable return period.
  • The product is not eligible for return due to applicable regulations.

MAAJ PHARMACY reserves the right to assess each return/refund request individually.
